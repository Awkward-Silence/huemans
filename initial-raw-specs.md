- web based app
- the main point is to encourange "breaking the ice" at any kind of event
- the real world problem we want to solve:
    - you are going to an event and you don't know anyone 
    - as an introvert it is difficult to talk to people - difficult to break the ice

application features:
  - QR code is an entry point 
  - once the QA is scanned, you get 20 seconds max to fill in a questinnaire
    - few questions, multiple choice
    - examples: colors (your preffered colors, do you like Julio Iglesios)
    - for the text answers, there will be pre-filled answers (10000+ funny questions database)
  - an algorithm will compute % matches with people around the event
  - the icebraker might happen in various ways - TBD
      - might be one sigle question for the whole evening - people can approach each other and ask themselves this question
      - extroverts might use a led-scroller like application where they put the phone above their head, in a full screen mode, showing their color
      - the app might ask people to gather at the same corner based on the color (all the reds in the next 5 minutes can meet in the beer corner)
      - you can chose to make a 1:1 match with someone at the event
  - befre doing matching, people might add to their profile their interest, tags that indicate the technigoliges etc and the app will show infographics about the people joining
  - the app encourouges matching with people after the event, at the after party or to continue discussing with them later
  - some people might know someone already - we can leverage that to introduce to other people
